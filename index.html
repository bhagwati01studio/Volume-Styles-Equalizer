1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Volume Styles & Equaliser (the “App”), developed by Bhagwati Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”), accesses, processes, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

The App is designed to let you personalise Android volume panels, preview volume-control styles, change appearance settings, and adjust supported audio controls. Some features may vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

2. Information we process

Volume and device settings

The App may access volume levels, sound-mode information, audio-output state, and other device settings required to display and operate its controls. These values are used to perform the requested functions on your device.

Preferences saved on your device

Settings such as selected volume-panel styles, themes, colours, position, favourites, animation preferences, and related customisation options may be stored locally so that your choices persist.

Advertising and technical information

If the App displays ads using Google AdMob, Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, including your IP address (which may indicate an approximate location), app interactions, diagnostic and performance data, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, where permitted and available. This information may support ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Purchases and support requests

If you purchase premium features or subscriptions, Google Play processes payment information. The App may receive purchase status and entitlement information to activate your benefits. If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and problem description.

Audio privacy: Customising a volume control does not itself require recording audio or conversations. The App is intended to use Android's audio-control interfaces rather than upload audio recordings to Bhagwati Studio servers.

3. Device permissions and access

Depending on the features available in your installed version, the App may request the following Android permissions or special access. A permission listed here does not mean that it is requested in every version.

AccessWhy it may be needed
Display over other appsTo show a customised volume panel above other apps when you adjust sound.
Accessibility serviceWhere implemented and explicitly enabled by you, to detect supported volume-control interactions and show the selected custom panel. The service may receive Android accessibility events. Its use must be limited to the functions explained in the App's separate accessibility disclosure.
Modify audio settingsTo read or adjust supported volume levels and sound settings when you interact with the controls.
Notifications or foreground serviceTo keep an enabled volume-control feature operating where Android requires an ongoing service or notification.
Internet accessFor enabled advertising, purchase verification, and any other online functionality expressly provided in the App.
Start on bootTo restore the selected volume-control experience after a device restart, if you enable that feature.

You can disable permissions in Android Settings. Some functions may stop working if their required access is turned off.

Accessibility disclosure: If the App uses the Android AccessibilityService API, it will provide a separate, prominent explanation and request your affirmative consent before enabling the feature. This Privacy Policy is not a substitute for that in-app disclosure.

5. How information is used and shared

Information may be processed for these purposes:

  • Operating and personalising volume controls and other features you select.
  • Remembering local app preferences and enabling purchased functionality.
  • Providing ads, measuring performance, preventing fraud, and complying with legal obligations where applicable.
  • Responding to support requests and maintaining the App's reliability and security.

We do not sell your personal information directly. Third-party advertising services may collect and use information independently as described in their privacy policies, and certain advertising disclosures or choices may be required under applicable privacy laws.

We may disclose information when legally required, to protect the App or its users, or to service providers acting on our behalf, only to the extent relevant to their functions.

6. Data retention and deletion

Preferences stored locally generally remain on your device until you reset the App, clear its data, or uninstall it. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily remove information separately held by Google, advertising providers, or your payment provider.

Support correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request and meet legitimate legal or operational needs. Advertising and purchase-related records are subject to the relevant provider's retention policies.

To request access to or deletion of information that Bhagwati Studio holds in connection with a support request, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

7. Your privacy choices

  • App customisation: Change or reset preferences inside the App, where available.
  • Special permissions: Revoke overlay, accessibility, and notification permissions in Android Settings.
  • Advertising preferences: Use any in-app privacy-options control provided, and manage available ad-privacy settings on your Android device.
  • Subscriptions: Manage or cancel applicable subscriptions in Google Play.
  • Local data: Clear app storage or uninstall the App to remove locally stored preferences.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of personal information. Contact us to exercise applicable rights concerning information under our control.

8. Data security

We aim to minimise data access and use Android's platform protections where possible. Services such as Google AdMob and Google Play apply their own safeguards to information they process. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required by applicable local law. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information directly. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.
